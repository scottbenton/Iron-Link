# Iron Link components

The redesign's component library, built from the Workbench exploration:
tactile flat surfaces, a gradient ring on roll and oracle buttons, condensed
headings, and quiet motion. It lives in `src/ui` beside MUI, so screens can
move over one at a time.

## How to look around

- **Mode and Scheme** in the toolbar switch light or dark and the nine colour
  schemes for every story at once.
- **Ring** switches how a stat card's ring appears: Fade (the default), Draw
  or Thicken.
- The **Accessibility** panel under each story runs axe on it.

## Sections

- **Foundations**: colour, type, space, radius, elevation, motion and the
  colour schemes.
- **Components**: general building blocks that replace MUI, such as buttons,
  fields, menus, dialogs, tabs, lists and toasts.
- **Game**: Iron Link's own pieces, such as stats, meters, progress tracks,
  clocks, roll cards, the game log, assets, moves, oracles and portraits.
- **Layout**: the app chrome, such as the nav bar and rail, page headers and
  the game page sidebar.

## Built on

- [Base UI](https://base-ui.com) for behaviour and accessibility.
- [Panda CSS](https://panda-css.com) for styles and tokens, with its
  preflight off so MUI is untouched.
- [Sonner](https://sonner.emilkowal.ski) to position and stack toasts, which
  render our own elements and never dismiss themselves.
- [Phosphor](https://phosphoricons.com) for icons.

Content in the stories comes from the Ironsworn and Starforged rules in
Datasworn, so moves, assets and oracles are the real thing.
